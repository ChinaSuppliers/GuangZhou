About Canton Fair

The Canton Fair in Guangzhou, China

A dedicated resource for international buyers, importers and businesses visiting the Canton Fair and the Canton Fair Complex in Guangzhou.

The Canton Fair, officially known as the China Import and Export Fair, is held in Guangzhou, Guangdong, China.

International buyers come to the Canton Fair to discover products, meet Chinese exhibitors, communicate with suppliers and explore potential business opportunities.

The Canton Fair Complex provides exhibition halls where companies from China present products and meet buyers from around the world.

For businesses interested in China sourcing, supplier communication and international trade, the Canton Fair provides an opportunity to meet companies directly.
